##  Project Overview
[Krusty Krab is a fast-food restaurant brand focused on serving delicious, high-quality meals in a fun, casual, and memorable way. The brand aims to create a strong identity that appeals to young people, families, and fast-food lovers while offering a convenient and enjoyable dining experience.

The project focuses on building a recognizable brand presence for Krusty Krab across digital and social media platforms, highlighting the restaurant’s food, personality, and unique customer experience. The goal is to position Krusty Krab as a fun and trustworthy fast-food choice and encourage customers to engage with the brand and place orders.]

---

## Project Objectives
- [Build a strong and memorable brand identity for Krusty Krab.]  
- [Increase brand awareness among the target audience.]  
- [Create engaging social media content that reflects the brand’s fun personality.]
